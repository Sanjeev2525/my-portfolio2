# AGENTS.md — Implementation Rules & Coding Conventions
**Project: Sanjeev A — Creative Developer Portfolio**

## 1. Core Philosophy
- This is Sanjeev A's personal portfolio. It is **not** a SaaS product, enterprise dashboard, or corporate agency site.
- **Simple content, complex craft**: concise, human storytelling with high interaction and visual polish.
- Never invent metrics, testimonials, company logos, awards, or personal life details.
- Never expose proprietary company architecture, secrets, or confidential internal systems from N51 Invest, Laabhum, or Gamepay.

## 2. Technical Standards
- Framework: Next.js 15 (App Router) + TypeScript + Tailwind CSS.
- Tokens: Strictly governed by `DESIGN.md`.
- Accessibility: Audited against `web-design-guidelines` (keyboard navigation, focus rings, semantic tags, reduced-motion).
- Bundle: Lightweight, zero heavy 3D or animation runtimes.
